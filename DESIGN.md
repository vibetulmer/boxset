# Design System: Boxset

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Box-Set Shelf"**

Boxset draws a lifetime of watched TV as a shelf of box sets, flat and graphic, on a Blu-ray cobalt ground. Every season is a spine coloured from its show's poster; a watched season is a filled spine with the show's name printed vertically, an unwatched one is an empty clear case: an outline with nothing printed on it. The gaps are the data. Numbers (hours, seasons, episodes) are set in an extra-condensed face and read like the spec block on the back of a case.

The surface is drenched, not decorated. Cobalt is the page, not an accent on white or black; depth comes from three tonal steps of the same blue (sunk, ground, raised) and from flat two-tone shelf boards, never from bevels, grain, sheen or ambient shadow. Colour carries meaning with discipline: yellow means watched and nothing else, white is the sticker colour for ratings, tiers and the primary action, and each show's own spine colour is the only chromatic variety on the page.

Density is moderate and phone-first: one-handed controls of 44px and up, divider-tab navigation that sits on top on desktop and hangs from the bottom edge on phone. The rejected direction is the streaming-clone poster grid on black with a red accent.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Blu-ray cobalt drench in three tonal steps; flat boards with a light top line and a cobalt face.
- Spines coloured from each poster, clamped so white lettering stays legible.
- Empty and partly watched seasons as outlined empty cases with no title.
- Yellow reserved for "watched"; white reserved for stickers, ratings, tiers and the primary action.
- Sofia Sans Extra Condensed, uppercase, for spines, figures, headings and labels; Sofia Sans for reading.
- One authored motion: the box set slides off the shelf into its detail page.

## Colors

A single-hue drench (cobalt, hue 255 to 267) with two reserved signal colours and per-show spine colours as the only free variety.

### Primary
- **Blu-ray Cobalt** (cobalt-ground): the page itself, `html` and `body` background, and the face that the active divider tab joins.
- **Sunk Cobalt** (cobalt-sunk): recessed surfaces: the header bar, mobile tab bar, search field, spec strip, segmented controls, steppers, progress-bar troughs, fact and data cards, the space behind tier boards.
- **Raised Cobalt** (cobalt-raised): lifted surfaces and hover fills: chips, notices, the pressed segmented option, outline-button hover, stepper hover, skeleton lines.
- **Board Face** and **Board Top** (board-face, board-top): the two flat bands of every shelf board (3px top line, cobalt face below, 16px total; 14px on phone). Board Top caps the stats stack.

### Secondary
- **Watched Yellow** (watched, with watched-hi for hover and watched-ink for text on it): the "watched" signal only. Watch button, ticked episode/season, rewatch-count tags on spines, progress-bar fill, the watch-count stepper value, the "done" badge, the user's own figures in the spec strip.

### Tertiary
- **Sticker White** (paper, with paper-ink for text on it): the sticker colour. Rating stickers on box sets and covers, selected rating segments, the S tier divider, the primary button, toasts, focus rings, text selection and the skip link.
- **Tier Fade** (tier-a to tier-d): the tier dividers fade from Sticker White (S) through progressively bluer, darker whites to tier-d, always with paper-ink text.
- **Spine colours** (runtime, per show): derived from the poster's dominant saturated colour and clamped to HSL saturation 40 to 75% and lightness 34 to 47%, so white spine lettering always holds; near-grey posters keep a muted tone. They paint spines, the stats stack bars, blank covers and the case behind a cover.

### Neutral
- **Ink** (ink): primary text and headings.
- **Ink 2** (ink-2): secondary text, ledes, summaries, the muted part of the ledger sentence.
- **Ink 3** (ink-3): tertiary text, inactive tabs, labels, metadata, icons in inputs.
- **Line / Line Soft** (line, line-soft): outlines on outline buttons and badges; hairline dividers between rows and sections.
- **Case Line** (case-line, 32% white): the outline of an empty clear case, rating segments at rest, unticked ticks.
- **Danger** (danger): destructive hover on quiet buttons and the error-notice outline only.

### Named Rules
**The Yellow Means Watched Rule.** Watched Yellow appears only where it states that something was watched (or how often). Never use it for emphasis, links, ratings or decoration.

**The Sticker Rule.** Sticker White marks judgement and the primary action: ratings, tiers, the primary button, toasts and focus. Inactive and navigational chrome stays in ink on cobalt.

**The Spine Owns Its Colour Rule.** Per-show colour lives only on objects that belong to that show (spine, stack bar, case, blank cover). Chrome never borrows a show's colour.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Sofia Sans Extra Condensed (registered as "Sofia Sans XC", with Sofia Sans, ui-sans-serif, system-ui)
**Body Font:** Sofia Sans (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui)

**Character:** The extra-condensed face is the printed lettering of a case: tall, uppercase, packed onto narrow spines and spec blocks. Sofia Sans is its readable sibling for sentences. Both are self-hosted variable fonts (weight 1 to 1000), latin and latin-ext subsets.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, clamp(2.25rem, 1.6rem + 3vw, 4.25rem), 0.92, uppercase, -0.01em, balanced): page titles (show name, Ranking, Statistik, Daten).
- **Headline** (800, clamp(1.875rem, 1.2rem + 2.8vw, 3.5rem), 1, uppercase): the lifetime sentence on the shelf and the stats head, set in ink-2 with the number in ink; max 20 to 22ch.
- **Title** (800, 1.375rem, 1.05, uppercase, 0.02em): section headings, search result names, season names (1.25rem).
- **Figure** (800, 1.625rem, 1, tabular figures): spec-strip values, fact figures (1.75rem), stepper values (1.75rem). Units and "ca." drop to 0.6em in ink-3.
- **Body** (400, 16px, 1.5): reading text. Lede at 1.0625rem in ink-2, max 60ch; summaries max 66ch.
- **Label** (700, 1rem, uppercase, 0.06em): tabs, segmented options (0.9375rem), badges (0.875rem). Buttons use the same face at 800, 1.0625rem, 0.05em.
- **Small label** (700, 0.75 to 0.8125rem, uppercase, 0.12em, ink-3): the name of a figure or control directly above it (spec-strip terms, control labels).
- **Spine** (700, 0.84rem, uppercase, 0.07em, vertical, read bottom to top): show names on spines; season numbers on spines at 800, 1rem, tabular.

### Named Rules
**The Condensed Speaks, Sans Reads Rule.** Anything that names, counts or labels (headings, figures, tabs, buttons, spines) is Sofia Sans Extra Condensed, uppercase. Anything read as a sentence is Sofia Sans, sentence case.

**The Tabular Figures Rule.** Every number that can change or be compared (counts, hours, episode numbers, ratings, progress) uses tabular figures.

**The Label Belongs To A Value Rule.** Small tracked uppercase labels exist only to name the figure or control directly beneath them. They never sit above a heading as a decorative tag.

## Layout

A single centred column, max 1180px, with a fluid gutter of clamp(16px, 4vw, 40px) and top padding of clamp(28px, 4vw, 52px). The shelf is a wrapping flex row of box sets aligned to the bottom, 14px apart horizontally (9px on phone) and one board (16px) apart vertically; the boards are painted as a repeating background so each wrapped row sits on its own board. Box-set height varies per show between 86 and 100% of the spine height, like real shelves.

Spine geometry is tokenised: 30 by 188px on desktop, 27 by 150px on phone; the detail page's large shelf uses 42 by 230px (36 by 190px on phone). Spines within a box set sit 2px apart.

Grouped blocks (spec strip, fact cards, data cards, stats stack, rating segments) are separated by a 3px seam of ground rather than borders or padding-heavy cards. Lists (search results, seasons, episodes) are separated by 1px soft hairlines.

Responsive: one breakpoint at 760px. Below it, the divider tabs move to a fixed bottom bar of four equal columns with icons above labels, the show header stacks, the rating scale fills the width, and tier dividers narrow from 132px to 78px.

**The Seam Rule.** Group related tiles with a 3px gap of ground inside a shared rounded container, not with borders or separate floating cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. Depth is tonal: Sunk Cobalt reads as recessed, Raised Cobalt as lifted, and the shelf boards are two flat bands. Outlines are inset 1.5px rings, never borders that shift layout. Hover lift is literal motion (a box set rises 8px off its board, a cover 5px, a large spine 6px), not shadow.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Toast float** (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px oklch(0.15 0.1 266 / 0.7)`): only the toast, the one element that floats above the page.

### Named Rules
**The No Fake Material Rule.** No bevels, gradients-as-lighting, grain, gloss or ambient card shadows. If it needs to look recessed, use Sunk Cobalt; if lifted, Raised Cobalt.

## Shapes

Small, object-true radii. Spines are nearly square (3px top, 1px bottom); covers 4px; badges 6px; buttons 7px; ticks and notice/spec blocks 8px; segmented controls and steppers 9px; search field, toasts and divider tabs 10px. Divider tabs round only their top corners on desktop and only their bottom corners on phone, so they always join the page edge. Chips are pills; rating stickers are circles rotated -8 to -10 degrees. The Blu-ray case frame around a face-out cover is a 7px translucent cobalt case with a short white logo band at the top.

Empty states are drawn as shape, not illustration: an empty case is a 1.5px inset Case Line outline with no fill and no title.

## Components

### Buttons
Condensed, uppercase and solid; they read as labels on the case.
- **Shape:** gently squared (7px), min-height 44px (38px small, 52px large).
- **Watch:** Watched Yellow with watched-ink; hover to watched-hi. Used for marking watched.
- **Primary:** Sticker White with paper-ink; hover to pure white.
- **Outline:** transparent with a 1.5px inset Line ring; hover raises to Raised Cobalt with an ink-3 ring.
- **Quiet:** ink-3 text, no fill; hover turns Danger (used for removal).
- **Press:** translateY(1px) scale(0.985); disabled at 45% opacity; busy pulses opacity.

### Chips
- **Style:** pills in Raised Cobalt with ink-2 text at 600, 0.9375rem; hover to Line Soft and ink. Used for starter suggestions.

### Segmented control
- **Style:** Sunk Cobalt track (9px, 3px padding) holding condensed uppercase options in ink-3; the pressed option fills Raised Cobalt with ink. Used for sort and filter.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Search:** 64px tall, Sunk Cobalt, 10px radius, no border, 1.5rem condensed text, search icon inset left in ink-3.
- **Focus:** a 2px inset Sticker White ring.
- **Stepper:** Sunk Cobalt with 48 by 52px icon buttons and a Watched Yellow figure between them (rewatch count).

### Navigation
- **Divider tabs:** condensed uppercase labels with icons, ink-3 on a faint 4% white fill inside the Sunk Cobalt header; hover to 8% and ink; the active tab fills with Blu-ray Cobalt so it joins the page below. On phone they become a fixed bottom bar, tabs hanging from the bottom edge.
- **Back link:** condensed uppercase, ink-3, 0.08em, hover to ink.

### Signature: Box set and spine
A box set is a row of spines, one per season, standing on a board. Filled spine: the show's colour, a darkened season-number cap (22% black), the show name vertical, and a yellow rewatch tag (e.g. "2x") when watched more than once. Partly watched spine: an empty case showing only the number and a yellow "watched/aired" tag at the foot. Empty spine: an empty case with only the number. A white rating sticker sits tilted at the top right of the box set. Newly added spines drop in from above (560ms).

### Signature: Spec strip
The back-of-case spec block: Sunk Cobalt, 8px radius, cells divided by a 1px Line Soft seam, each with a small label over a Figure. The user's own figures turn Watched Yellow.

### Signature: Tier boards
Each tier is a white-to-blue divider card (top-left 10px radius) with a huge condensed letter (4rem, 3rem on phone) and its label, beside a board holding face-out cases with rating stickers.

### Signature: Watch controls
Ticks are 40px (32px small) empty cases with a 1.5px Case Line ring; ticked, they fill Watched Yellow with a check. The rating scale is ten 36 by 52px case segments; selected values fill Sticker White and the chosen one lifts 4px.

### Toast
Sticker White bar, 10px radius, the only floating shadow, with a paper-ink condensed action button for undo.

### Motion
One authored transition: a View Transition named "boxset" carries the clicked box set from the shelf into the detail page's large shelf (480ms, cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)); the root cross-fades in 220ms. Everything else is state feedback on the same ease-out curve, 140 to 320ms. All motion collapses under prefers-reduced-motion.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep the page drenched in Blu-ray Cobalt and express depth with Sunk and Raised Cobalt only.
- **Do** show unwatched seasons as outlined empty cases (1.5px inset Case Line, no fill, no title).
- **Do** use Watched Yellow only to say "watched" or "how often watched".
- **Do** use Sticker White for ratings, tiers, the primary action, toasts and focus rings.
- **Do** set names, numbers, tabs and buttons in Sofia Sans Extra Condensed uppercase with tabular figures; set sentences in Sofia Sans.
- **Do** group tiles with a 3px seam of ground inside one rounded container.
- **Do** derive per-show colour from the poster and clamp it so white lettering stays legible.
- **Do** keep controls at 44px or larger on phone and keep the tab bar at the bottom there.

### Don't:
- **Don't** build a streaming-clone poster grid on black with a red accent.
- **Don't** add bevels, grain, sheen, gloss or ambient card shadows; the only shadow is the toast's.
- **Don't** use yellow for emphasis, links or ratings.
- **Don't** print a title on an empty or partly watched case.
- **Don't** put small tracked uppercase tags above headings; small labels only name the value beneath them.
- **Don't** add a second authored transition; the box set sliding into its page is the one moment.
